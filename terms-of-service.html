Any modifications or feature additions outside the documented SOW are handled via formal written change requests.

3. Milestone Payment Escrow Protocol

To protect both parties, BlazeByte Studio operates on structured milestone billing:

  • Project Initiation Deposit: A standard 50% advance deposit is payable prior to architecture discovery, sprint planning, and repository setup.
  • Staged Delivery Review: Work is deployed to a password-protected staging environment for client review and verification.
  • Final Settlement & Release: The remaining 50% milestone balance is payable strictly upon verification of agreed deliverables, triggering immediate production deployment and full source code handover.
  • Monthly Marketing Retainers: Invoiced in advance on a recurring monthly cycle, cancellable with 14 business days' written notice prior to next renewal.
