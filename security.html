Security, Privacy & Commercial Protections

How BlazeByte Studio protects client data, enforces zero-retention AI guardrails, safeguards source code intellectual property, and guarantees commercial confidentiality.

OWASP Top 10 Software Security

Every custom application and web platform is engineered following strict OWASP Top 10 guidelines. We enforce parameterized queries against SQL injection, strict context-aware output encoding against cross-site scripting (XSS), anti-CSRF token verification, and cryptographically secure session handling.

✓ INPUT SANITIZATION • STRICT TYPES • SECURE HEADERS

Zero-Retention AI Data Privacy

When implementing custom AI chatbots, RAG pipelines, or LLM integrations, we strictly utilize enterprise API endpoints with zero-data-retention terms. Your organizational documents, customer inquiries, and proprietary knowledge bases are never used to retrain public foundation models.

✓ ZERO TRAINING LOGS • ISOLATED VECTOR INDEXES • STRICT RAG

100% Client Source Code IP Ownership

You own your software. Upon final milestone settlement, complete copyright, repository commits, application assets, and database schemas are formally transferred to your entity. We never claim proprietary lock-in or licensing royalties on custom software developed for you.

Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

Before sharing sensitive business logic, API secrets, or product specifications, we execute a legally binding mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement upon request. All senior engineers and specialists assigned to your project are bound by strict international confidentiality agreements.

✓ MUTUAL LEGAL NDA • PRE-DISCOVERY SIGNING AVAILABLE

PCI-DSS Level 1 Payment Processing

We never store, process, or view client credit card numbers or banking secrets on our servers. All international transactions and retainers are settled through world-class PCI-DSS Level 1 certified processors (Stripe and Razorpay) utilizing end-to-end tokenization and 3D Secure 2 authentication.

✓ STRIPE • RAZORPAY • 3D SECURE 2 TOKENIZATION

Production web applications and client platforms are deployed behind TLS 1.3 / SSL encrypted layers with modern HTTP security headers (CSP, HSTS, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy). Edge distribution networks protect endpoints against volumetric DDoS intrusions.

Security & Data Protection FAQs

Can we sign an NDA prior to discussing project requirements?

Yes. We regularly execute bilateral or unilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements with international clients before requirements workshops, API audits, or codebase reviews. You can request our standard international mutual NDA or provide your legal team's template.

Are AI chatbot interactions recorded or trained on?

No. Our production AI chatbots and workflow automation systems connect through enterprise-grade API connections that enforce strict zero-data-retention policies. Customer conversations, uploaded PDFs, and organizational knowledge remain completely private and isolated.

Who retains ownership of the codebase after delivery?

You own 100% of the custom source code, documentation, and digital assets once final milestone payment is completed. You receive full administrative access to GitHub/GitLab repositories and hosting servers.

How are international client payments secured?

We invoice through Stripe and Razorpay International, supporting USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD, and AED. Payments are processed over encrypted channels with 3D Secure 2 authentication and automated receipt generation.
